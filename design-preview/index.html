02 / HOW I WORK

Curiosity is good.
Follow-through is better.

My job often begins when a product experience doesn't quite do what people need. Here's the route I take from problem to shipped work.

01 / LISTEN◉

Find the actual problem.

Review customer feedback, search patterns, requests from Sales, and what's happening inside the product.

02 / RESEARCH⌕

Do the homework.

Study domain standards and competitors, document the gaps, and scope what a practical solution needs.

03 / BUILD✳

Make it useful.

Create and organize assets, test them in staging, collaborate on priorities, and support the release.

04 / LEARN↗

Check what happened.

Measure real usage, question the assumptions, and look for ways to make the experience better.
