THE THINKING BEHIND THE COPY

It's not enough to list what the software does.

01 / RESEARCH

Understand the choice.

Look at the competing products, their intended audiences, and the workflows a prospective customer is trying to accomplish.

02 / POSITIONING

Explain the difference.

Connect features to real use cases. A list of capabilities is more useful when readers can see how each one affects their work.

03 / WRITING

Make it easy to evaluate.

Use approachable structure and specific explanations so a reader can compare options without digging through dozens of unrelated product pages.
