THE FULL PROFESSIONAL PICTURE

I'm a Product Content Specialist at SmartDraw. My work sits across product research, content and UX, customer needs, and positioning—but most days, I'm focused on one question: how do we make this more useful?

VERIFIED EXPERIENCE / 01

More than a library of shapes.

SmartDraw lets people create professional diagrams and scaled technical drawings without needing to become CAD experts. The symbols, templates, and examples people start from are a major part of that experience, and I help create and maintain them.

My job often starts far away from Illustrator. I research customer feedback, competitor products, standards, and how professionals actually work. Then I decide what kind of content would solve the problem, build a plan, and do the hands-on work to ship it.

For bigger efforts, I document the problem, evidence, research, scope, and examples in Confluence and review the proposal with my Product Manager. For smaller improvements, I have room to make the call and move. I don't manage Engineering or own SmartDraw's roadmap, but my research and recommendations inform what we prioritize.

VERIFIED EXPERIENCE / 02

I'll read the long document.

Some days I'm figuring out how HVAC drawings are conventionally built. Others, I'm studying NFPA documents, OSHA requirements, forensic references, or competitor interfaces. I don't expect to know every industry on day one. I'm good at learning enough to make useful product decisions.

There's a practical side, too. When I reorganized Azure icons, I mirrored Microsoft's category structure instead of creating another hard-to-maintain “New Shapes” collection. When floor-plan libraries were cluttered with duplicate furniture across rooms, I moved toward furniture types and clearer residential/commercial groupings.

I also work with Sales, Customer Success, Product, and Marketing. I've handled customer conversations directly, including covering customer success responsibilities when the team needed help. I enjoy it when a product improvement gives those teams a better answer to a real customer question.

VERIFIED EXPERIENCE / 03

I don't stop after the proposal.

Most of my projects don't need an Engineering build. I create the symbols, templates, SVG assets, and library structure within SmartDraw's existing capabilities. For detailed assets, I draw in Adobe Illustrator, then prepare them for use and customization in the product.

I also verify the content in QA and staging environments, check customization and scale, and run another integrity pass after production deployment. Another team manages the actual deployments. If I find a content issue, I fix the source and follow it through the release process.

I'm comfortable using Jira, Illustrator, Figma, AutoCAD, CMS tools, Search Console, and Keyword Planner. For product usage questions, I use Claude, DuckDB, and SQL queries to investigate what customers search for and how they use templates.
