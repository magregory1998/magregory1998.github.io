BEHIND THE WORK / MATTHEW GREGORY

Curious by nature. Hands-on by habit.

I research specialized products, figure out what people are trying to accomplish, and turn gaps into content they can actually use. My title is Product Content Specialist, but the work crosses research, product design, launch support, and usage analysis.

A little background.

SmartDraw since February 2022. Based in Greater Houston.

01 / WHAT I DO

I start with a question, not a finished brief.

My work typically begins with customer feedback, competitor research, a domain standard, or something in the product that could work better. I document the business rationale, scope the content, and collaborate with our product manager on priorities.

Once approved, I develop and organize the assets, test them in staging and production, and help Sales and Marketing communicate the release. I don't own SmartDraw's entire product roadmap or its marketing campaigns, but I'm comfortable making complex work understandable across both sides.

02 / HOW I THINK

There's usually a better question to ask.

What are customers actually trying to make? Why does this feel harder than it should? Which missing symbols or templates would help them? Can we measure whether people use what we ship?

I value accurate answers more than neat-looking ones. If the evidence doesn't support a conclusion, I'd rather say so and keep investigating.

03 / WHAT I ENJOY

Make it good. Then make it useful.

I enjoy the overlap between research and craft: learning a new technical domain, shaping a content system that makes sense, drawing the individual pieces, and seeing them become tools people can use.

04 / OUTSIDE WORK

There's more to life than a screen.

Outside of work, I'm a husband, a dad, and an enthusiastic (although admittedly average) fisherman. I enjoy spending time with my family, listening to music, and picking up new hobbies whenever something catches my interest.

I've always been a curious person. I love learning new things, asking questions, and occasionally getting lost down a rabbit hole on a topic I knew nothing about the day before. I appreciate a good sense of humor, meaningful conversations, and people who don't take themselves too seriously.

More than anything, I value my family, enjoy the little things in life, and try to approach whatever I'm doing with an open mind and a willingness to learn.
